Why the space is checked

Several of the measures in this report are not computed on the text directly. They are computed on a numerical representation of each post, and the choice of representation is a judgement rather than a detail. The risk the project identified at the outset is that a general representation may arrange posts by how they are written rather than by what they are about, in which case a measure that appears to compare subject matter is really comparing tone.

The check is to measure the same posts in several representations and see what survives. Nothing about the generated posts changes between runs. Only the representation they are measured in changes. Six representations are compared: the offline word-count default, a second sentence-encoder family, and one encoder at four sizes.

  • TF-IDF + SVD at 256 dimensions. Word counts weighted by rarity and then compressed. It is fitted on this corpus, needs no download, and knows nothing about language beyond which words co-occur here. This is the offline default the results pages use.
  • EmbeddingGemma, a trained sentence encoder, at 128, 256, 512 and 768 dimensions. It carries general knowledge of meaning and places posts near one another when they are about the same thing, whether or not they share vocabulary. The four sizes test whether a conclusion is stable within one encoder.
  • MiniLM-L6 at 384 dimensions. A second, unrelated encoder family, included so that agreement is not a coincidence of one model.

Does the score separate the baselines?

The harness has a built-in test for whether a triangle score is measuring content or merely register. The shuffle control is real Reddit posts on the wrong topic. A score that measures subject matter must rank it among the worst columns; a score that responds only to register ranks it among the best, because its register is already correct. The rule is that a score which cannot separate the baselines this way must not be reported.

The word-count representation fails this test: it ranks the wrong-topic control second best, above all three rewriters, which means its triangle score is largely reading register rather than subject. Every neural representation ranks the wrong-topic control worst, where a content-sensitive score should, with EmbeddingGemma at 256 dimensions the one marginal case. This is the reason the results pages report the neural triangle score at 512 dimensions rather than 256.

Which conclusions survive

Of the 10 comparisons the report states, 7 reach the same verdict in every representation. 2 hold under the word-count default and under no neural representation: “claude rewriting degrades distribution match relative to the source”; “claude rewriting raises self-similarity above the authentic reddit level”. Both rest on the triangle score in the one representation that fails the baseline test above, so both are properties of that representation rather than of the posts, and neither is a result.

The withdrawn distribution-match claim, in detail

The gap between the untouched source and the Claude rewrite is large and its intervals separate only under the word-count representation. In every neural representation, at every size and in both model families, the gap shrinks to within noise. More dimensions do not recover it, so the effect is not merely under-measured at 256 dimensions; it was specific to the space that fails the baseline test.

How each measure moves

The remaining figures compare the word-count default against the neural reference (Gemma 512-d) directly. Both runs cover exactly the same cells on every measure, so the two sets of numbers are averaged over the same posts. Of the measures in the results table, 8 are computed in the representation and 7 are not. The 7 that are not come out identical in both runs, which confirms the runs differ in the representation and in nothing else. Those measures are reads like reddit, share judged reddit, keeps the specific details, writing-habit mismatch, writing habits reproduced, copied the source outright, variety of phrasing used.

The values themselves are expected to differ, because the two representations are different scales and were never comparable. What matters is whether the columns come out in the same order, since the order is what a conclusion rests on. The order changes on 6 of the 8 measures.

How much of the score was writing style

Every measure computed in a representation is also computed a second time with the two directions that best separate LinkedIn from Reddit removed. The difference between the two is an estimate of how much of the score was responding to writing style rather than to subject matter.

In both representations the removal moves the score very little relative to the score itself, so neither appears to be measuring writing style to any great degree. This estimate is a lower bound in both representations, because two straight-line directions cannot capture everything that makes a post read as one platform or the other, and it is measured along different directions in each. The baseline-separation test above is the more direct check, and it is what the word-count representation fails.

Reading them together

The triangle score is defined relative to the distance it is computed over, so a value from one representation cannot be compared with a value from another at all. It also ranks the columns differently across representations, so a ranking it produces is a statement about the representation and not only about the posts. Reports state the representation alongside the value for this reason, and the harness files a report under the representation that produced it so that two cannot be mistaken for one another.

The subject-matter measure is a second clear case. Measured in the word-count representation, 2 of the 3 rewritten columns score no better than the control that was handed the wrong topic entirely. That reads as complete topical failure, and inspection of the outputs contradicts it. The measure is computed over groupings of the representation, so it appears to have been detecting machine authorship as much as topic drift. Measured in the neural representations, none of them does. The earlier reading was a property of the representation rather than of the posts.
