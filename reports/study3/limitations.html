Limitations

The conditions under which the results above hold, and the claims they do not support.

Prompt structure across trained methods

LoRA, soft prompting and steering place the shared rendered context in the same base-model chat template at fitting and inference. Steering also receives the complete source post, so wrapping and source truncation are held constant across the three mechanisms.

The primary comparison base is 11 cells

Each metric family restricts every method column to one shared cell set, but minimum pool sizes make those sets different. The Triangle-Rank Metric covers 11 cells, distributional and structural measures cover 16, and the per-post semantic, classifier and degeneracy measures cover 25. Resampling is over cells rather than posts, since posts within one cell are not exchangeable with posts in another. The primary intervals are correspondingly wide. LoRA against the soft prompt is not resolved on the Triangle-Rank Metric, and the report does not order them.

The authentic-post oracle covers 6 cells on the primary measure

The systems-only report excludes the oracle from the common-cell intersection so that its limited coverage does not reduce the method comparison. Identity and the wrong-topic control are therefore drawn from the systems-only report on the same 11-cell base as the methods. The oracle is overlaid from the full report and covers 6 cells on the Triangle-Rank Metric. Its rule is a calibration marker rather than a like-for-like aggregate, and every figure label and table cell states that base.

The unbiased MMD estimator is reported but not used here

The report carries an unbiased squared MMD (distributional.mmd2) computed as the U-statistic of Gretton et al., in which the diagonal terms are removed to eliminate the O(1/n) bias. That statistic is unbiased for a non-negative population quantity and is consequently not itself non-negative: on pools whose true MMD² is near zero it takes negative values with probability approaching one half, which is what the oracle column (target_sample) exhibits. The V-statistic form retains the diagonal, equals the squared norm of the difference of empirical kernel mean embeddings, and is non-negative by construction, at the cost of a positive bias that is severe at these pool sizes. On this corpus the guard MIN_MMD_POOL = 5 leaves the row scored on too few cells to support a comparison, so the tables above report centroid distance (distributional.centroid_distance) instead and the MMD row should not be read as a result.

Selection is isolated from the scored reference pool

Early stopping and hyperparameter selection use a deterministic train slice defined by a 0.15 blake2b(post_id, seed) hash threshold. Validation and test are untouched references, so this study can score their merged heldout pool without selection leakage.

The selected corpus does not support a bilateral job-title comparison

Subreddit labels are present for 559 of 559 Reddit posts, but job-title labels are present for 0 of 553 LinkedIn posts. The job-title and subreddit infusion rows lack the shared audience-room mapping needed to compare matched populations across platforms. Study 3 therefore evaluates the existing audience-by-topic cells and does not report a job-title or subreddit subpopulation result.

Steering is reported at a selected strength, and the selection is consequential

At the unselected default strength the offset produced degenerate output: repetitive fragments continuing to the generation limit, with vocabulary variety (degeneracy.distinct_2) far below every other column. All three steering columns would then have been read as a failure of the method rather than of a default. The layer and strength reported here were selected on the train-internal selection slice by maximising the cosine between the generated and authentic pool centroids subject to a degeneracy constraint, with the selected strengths between one tenth and one half of that default. Steering nonetheless does not reach the level the other two trained families reach on the triangle score (trm.trm), and that result should be read as applying to activation steering at its best available setting on this corpus, under this generation configuration, rather than to the method in general.

Length accounts for much of the effect, and is partly a generation setting

The largest single difference between the families is output length (degeneracy.length_ratio_vs_real). For the soft prompt and for LoRA this is learned from the target distribution. For steering it is not: the base model does not emit an end-of-sequence token under this prompt and continues to max_new_tokens, so steering's length ratio reflects the decoding configuration rather than the intervention. An additive offset in the residual stream alters the conditional distribution over tokens without altering the model's stopping behaviour, and a comparison that isolated the intervention would require equalising that behaviour across families.

Aspect conditioning rests on vocabularies built with the platform visible

The aspect vocabularies were extracted with platform labels visible to the extracting model, so the vocabulary may have been constructed to separate the platforms, which is the same signal the aspect-conditioned columns are then scored on. A blind rerun is pending. Every aspect-conditioned result is provisional until it lands. Coverage is a second constraint: vocabularies join to 16 of the cells present in training, and the aspect-aware prompting column ran unconditioned on roughly half its outputs.

TRM is not a topic or aspect score

The Triangle-Rank Metric compares complete generated and authentic distributions in google/embeddinggemma-300m, dim 512 cosine space. Removing two measured platform directions leaves the primary ordering nearly unchanged, which shows only that those two directions do not explain the result. It does not establish that TRM is dominated by topic, and it cannot determine whether the intended evaluative aspects were transferred.

Scraped text is normalised before scoring

The v2 rebuild decodes HTML entities before splitting or scoring. This removes the one-sided token artefact present in the Study 2 inputs.

Two directions, one base model and one primary embedding space

Study 3 evaluates LinkedIn to Reddit and Reddit to LinkedIn on independent comparison bases. The values are not directly subtractable because each direction has a different target distribution and reference pool. All trained families still share one base model, and the primary findings in each direction use one embedding space.
