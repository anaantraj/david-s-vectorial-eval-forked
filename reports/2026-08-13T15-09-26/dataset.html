Dataset

The corpus, the cell structure, the split rule, and the three conditionings of the target text used to fit the adaptation methods. This page is self-contained; nothing on it requires the methods page.

Corpus and cell structure

The corpus pairs an occupational audience with a topic to form a bilateral cell, a cell being retained only where both platforms carry at least 5 posts. The harness holds 1112 posts in 33 such cells, of which 5 are dense, meaning at least 10 posts on each platform. Coverage, rather than corpus size, is the binding constraint: of roughly fifteen hundred topic clusters in the source data, only these 33 carry sufficient mass on both platforms.

Source platform is linkedin and target platform is reddit. Of the 1112 posts, 553 are drawn from the source platform and 559 from the target.

Splits

Splits are assigned at the level of the post, stratified within a cell and platform, in the proportions 70%, 15% and 15%. Assignment is blake2b(post_id, seed) rather than a shuffle, so the mapping is a pure function of the identifier and appending newly scraped posts never reshuffles an existing assignment. A cell-level split was rejected because cells hold between five and roughly one hundred and fifty posts, and every metric in the harness compares pools; a cell-level split would leave single-digit pools.

Leakage is controlled by role rather than by partition. A task's exemplars are drawn only from train, while the authentic posts it is scored against come from the split being scored. Separately, 5 whole cells are withheld from train and validation entirely, which provides a zero-shot set for topics from which no exemplar has ever been seen.

This study is scored on the merged validation-and-test heldout split. Model selection uses only a deterministic slice carved from train, so neither part of the scored reference pool informed selection.

The trainable projection

There is no paired data. No source-platform post in this corpus has a corresponding target-platform post, which is why the harness compares pools rather than pairs and why no generated post has a gold reference. A trained method therefore cannot be fitted to source-and-target examples without first inventing them, and inventing them with a language model would fit the study to another model's opinion of the answer, which the evaluation would then measure agreement with.

Each method is instead fitted as a conditional language model over authentic train-split target-platform posts, with the source post supplied at inference as content to be carried across. Restricting the projection to target-platform posts leaves 311 training examples over 28 cells, with 72 for selection and 176 held out. That figure is the entire training signal behind every trained column in this study and belongs in any reading of the results. It is also small enough that LoRA overfitting was the expected outcome; it did not occur, and every run improved on the base model before early stopping engaged.

The three conditionings

The completion is always the authentic post, verbatim. The variants differ only in what conditions it, and all three share one prompt template, so a method may be fitted to each without anything changing but the file it reads.

topic onlyaudience, domain and topic 31172176 28
retrieved sourcethe above, plus the nearest same-cell source post under TF-IDF cosine 31172176 28
aspectsthe topic fields, plus the evaluative dimensions the post foregrounds 169 31 103 16

Topic only presents no source post during fitting, so the content block supplied at inference is structurally unseen. Retrieved source removes that mismatch by pairing each target with the nearest same-cell source post, but the pairing is weak by construction: a retrieved pair averages 0.137 cosine against 0.079 for a random same-cell pair, and fewer than one per cent reach 0.3. Retrieval recovers topical neighbours because the corpus contains no content correspondences to recover, so the variant supplies the shape of the inference context rather than a supervision signal for content preservation. Aspects abandons content transfer as the objective and conditions on the evaluative dimensions the target post emphasises; its context and completion are genuinely coupled, unlike the other two, at the cost of coverage, since aspect vocabularies join to 16 of the cells present in training.

Text normalisation

HTML entities are decoded once during the v2 dataset build, before any split, fit or score is computed.
