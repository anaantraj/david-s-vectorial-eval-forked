How to read the direction comparison

The forward and reverse values must not be treated as columns in one ranking. Each direction has its own authentic target pool and reference anchors. Agreement in the winning family is evidence of directional stability; a different ordering is evidence that the intervention depends on which platform is being modelled. Neither permits subtracting the two Triangle-Rank values as an effect size.
