Caveats

What this build cannot support

Two column families are known not to measure what their names say, and the study's scope is narrower than its plan.

Known defects in this build

Two families of columns do not measure what their names say. Both are reproduced and understood; neither is fixed in this build, and no claim resting on them is safe to quote.

Soft-prompt columns collapse to their Stage A adapter

The three soft_prompt_s4_soft_* systems generate byte-identical text despite loading three genuinely different prompt-embedding tensors (32 × 4096, maximum elementwise difference 0.62). All three declare the same initial_adapter. In transfer/soft_prompt.py the Stage A adapter is attached with PeftModel.from_pretrained and the prompt-tuning artifact is then attached to that already-wrapped model, so generation runs through the inner adapter and the virtual tokens are never prepended. The output is the Stage A LoRA alone. The soft_prompt_s4_base_soft_* columns declare no initial adapter and are not affected by this path.

Every steering column is an unsteered baseline

Selection returned alpha = 0 at layer = 0 for lora_interaction, target_lm, target_lm_aspect, target_lm_paired, so the intervention is the identity and each steering column is its base model under the steering prompt template. This is a selection outcome rather than a crash: no steered grid point beat the null under the degeneracy-constrained objective. It is reportable as “steering did not help”, but not as a comparison between steering variants, which are all the same null run.

The reverse direction is generating from the same code path and will need the same two corrections before its soft-prompt and steering columns can be read.

Unseen topics, aspects and traits

The held-out-cell split contains 5 forward and 5 reverse topic cells. These cells remain inside the reported held-out evaluation, but this four-audience case study does not support a separate audience-generalisation claim. Positive aspect labels cover 379 of 750 training posts. Aspect-conditioned columns are exploratory because the coverage and human-validation gates were not met. Trait steering was not run because no supplied trait contrast passed those gates.

Limitations

  • No cross-platform person linkage exists.
  • The audience mapping is a measurement assumption.
  • Collection timestamps do not establish one common observation window across both platforms.
  • Upstream profile summaries, keywords and dimensions are weak labels rather than human-validated traits.
  • Whole-room trait summaries include held-out authors, so they remain audit-only rather than conditioning the fitted models.
  • There is no gold rewrite for an individual source post.
  • Twitter and Quora were not present in the supplied corpus and are not reported as completed replications.
  • Aspect-conditioned results remain exploratory because the human-validation gate was not met.
