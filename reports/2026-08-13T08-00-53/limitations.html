Limitations

The conditions under which the results above hold, and the claims they do not support.

The steering columns are confounded with the prompt wrapping

The three steering artifacts are fitted and generate on the raw rendered context produced by render_prompt. The LoRA and soft-prompt methods apply the base model's chat template to that same context, at fitting and at inference alike. The steering columns therefore differ from the other two trained families in two respects at once, the intervention and the wrapping, and any difference between them compounds the two. This is recorded in the steering run records and is not inferred here.

The consequence is specific and it is not symmetric. Comparisons of steering against the prompted columns remain interpretable, since neither applies the template in the same way the trained families do, but no claim in this report about steering relative to LoRA or to the soft prompt is a measurement of the intervention. Refitting the three artifacts under the same wrapping removes the confound and is the first thing that should be done before these columns are cited. A second, smaller asymmetry compounds it: the steering function truncates the source post to 3000 characters before rendering, where the other two pass it whole.

The primary comparison base is 5 cells

Each metric family restricts every method column to one shared cell set, but minimum pool sizes make those sets different. The Triangle-Rank Metric covers 5 cells, distributional and structural measures cover 8, and the per-post semantic, classifier and degeneracy measures cover 17. Resampling is over cells rather than posts, since posts within one cell are not exchangeable with posts in another. The primary intervals are correspondingly wide. LoRA against the soft prompt is not resolved on the Triangle-Rank Metric, and the report does not order them.

The authentic-post oracle covers 1 cell on the primary measure

The systems-only report excludes the oracle from the common-cell intersection so that its limited coverage does not reduce the method comparison. Identity and the wrong-topic control are therefore drawn from the systems-only report on the same 5-cell base as the methods. The oracle is overlaid from the full report and covers 1 cell on the Triangle-Rank Metric. Its rule is a calibration marker rather than a like-for-like aggregate, and every figure label and table cell states that base.

The unbiased MMD estimator is reported but not used here

The report carries an unbiased squared MMD (distributional.mmd2) computed as the U-statistic of Gretton et al., in which the diagonal terms are removed to eliminate the O(1/n) bias. That statistic is unbiased for a non-negative population quantity and is consequently not itself non-negative: on pools whose true MMD² is near zero it takes negative values with probability approaching one half, which is what the oracle column (target_sample) exhibits. The V-statistic form retains the diagonal, equals the squared norm of the difference of empirical kernel mean embeddings, and is non-negative by construction, at the cost of a positive bias that is severe at these pool sizes. On this corpus the guard MIN_MMD_POOL = 5 leaves the row scored on too few cells to support a comparison, so the tables above report centroid distance (distributional.centroid_distance) instead and the MMD row should not be read as a result.

The trained methods select on part of the pool they are scored against, unless scored on test

Early stopping and hyperparameter selection use the validation split. The harness's usual scored split merges validation and test, so a trained method scored there would have selected against roughly half of its own reference pool, while the untrained baselines would have no such advantage. This study is therefore scored on test alone, which removes the dependence. The cost is a smaller cell base, and that cost is the reason the previous section reads as it does.

Steering is reported at a selected strength, and the selection is consequential

At the unselected default strength the offset produced degenerate output: repetitive fragments continuing to the generation limit, with vocabulary variety (degeneracy.distinct_2) far below every other column. All three steering columns would then have been read as a failure of the method rather than of a default. The layer and strength reported here were selected on the validation split by maximising the cosine between the generated and authentic pool centroids subject to a degeneracy constraint, with the selected strengths between one tenth and one half of that default. Steering nonetheless does not reach the level the other two trained families reach on the triangle score (trm.trm), and that result should be read as applying to activation steering at its best available setting on this corpus, under this generation configuration, rather than to the method in general.

Length accounts for much of the effect, and is partly a generation setting

The largest single difference between the families is output length (degeneracy.length_ratio_vs_real). For the soft prompt and for LoRA this is learned from the target distribution. For steering it is not: the base model does not emit an end-of-sequence token under this prompt and continues to max_new_tokens, so steering's length ratio reflects the decoding configuration rather than the intervention. An additive offset in the residual stream alters the conditional distribution over tokens without altering the model's stopping behaviour, and a comparison that isolated the intervention would require equalising that behaviour across families.

Aspect conditioning rests on vocabularies built with the platform visible

The aspect vocabularies were extracted with platform labels visible to the extracting model, so the vocabulary may have been constructed to separate the platforms, which is the same signal the aspect-conditioned columns are then scored on. A blind rerun is pending. Every aspect-conditioned result is provisional until it lands. Coverage is a second constraint: vocabularies join to 17 of the cells present in training, and the aspect-aware prompting column ran unconditioned on roughly half its outputs.

These numbers were computed before the scraped text was cleaned

The corpus carried HTML entities left by the scrape, and every post carrying one is a Reddit post, so the encoded entity was a token appearing on one platform and never the other. The platform classifier is free to key on it. The dataset builder now decodes them, and the fix applies from the next rebuild onward, so the classifier rows here carry that artefact. It is bounded by the five per cent of posts involved and it inflates apparent separability rather than hiding it.

One direction, one base model, one embedding space

Everything here is LinkedIn to Reddit. The reverse direction is supported and unexamined. All three trained families share one base model, so nothing separates the mechanism from that model's particular behaviour. And every embedding-derived row is computed in one space; the first study found that seven of ten comparisons held across six spaces while two did not, so single-space results should be treated as provisional until replicated.
