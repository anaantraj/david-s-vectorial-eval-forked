Prior corpora

Reconstruction from the exports

The exports mix production, test, cloned, merged and repeated rooms, and their profile histories overlap the scored splits. The corpus builder therefore resolves canonical exports, removes duplicates, groups every record by its platform author, excludes any profile that overlaps the scored splits, and only then assigns authors to splits. The policies it applied are recorded in the manifests and reproduced here.

Split unit
author_id. Authors, not posts, are assigned to splits, so no author contributes to both a training and a development set.
Rung selection
nested deterministic blake2b over record_id. Each smaller rung is a subset of the larger one, so the scale ladder compares data size and not sample composition.
Cross-room policy
one deterministic primary audience plus all audience_memberships.
Held-out exclusion rule
drop the complete S3 profile when any post ID or normalized URL matches Study 3 val/test (Reddit manifest); 69 Reddit and 38 LinkedIn profiles were excluded under this rule.
Weak-label policy
retained only on train records; dev receives only frozen train-author aggregates.
Derived fields
retained as structured fields and never used as language-model targets.
Collection cutoff
2026-08-20 (Reddit manifest); the LinkedIn manifest records no cutoff. A common observation window across the two platforms is therefore not established.

Structured priors

Shipped profile summaries, keywords, dimensions and room traits are weak labels rather than validated annotations. Only labels tied to profiles assigned wholly to the training split may condition training; whole-room summaries and traits in the supplied export include held-out authors and are audit-only.
